Good to know

Your questions, answered.

We're building Majorglory Travels with clarity and convenience in mind.

Can I book flights right now?

Not yet. The website is currently a preview. Live airline inventory, checkout and ticket issuance will be activated only after supplier and payment integrations are tested and approved.

Is Majorglory Travels only for Mpumalanga residents?

No. We're starting with Mpumalanga-focused travel experiences, but travellers from anywhere will be welcome.

Will I be able to book accommodation?

Yes, accommodation is part of our planned offering, subject to a working supplier integration.

What about bus tickets?

Bus bookings are on our roadmap and require a separate authorised transport inventory provider.

Something exciting is on the horizon.

Majorglory Travels is preparing a new way to explore, plan and book.
